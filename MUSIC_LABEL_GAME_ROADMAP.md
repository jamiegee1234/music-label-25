# Music Label Management Game - Complete Roadmap

## Game Overview
A comprehensive music industry simulation game inspired by Football Manager, where players build and manage their own record label from a small indie startup to a major industry powerhouse.

## Core Game Mechanics

### 1. Label Progression System
- **Startup Phase**: Small budget, local artists, basement studio
- **Independent Phase**: Regional success, better equipment, small venues
- **Major Phase**: National/international reach, stadium tours, celebrity artists
- **Mogul Phase**: Multi-label empire, catalog acquisitions, industry influence

### 2. Artist Development Pipeline

#### Scouting & Discovery
- **Talent Scouts**: Hire scouts with different specialties (genres, regions)
- **Discovery Methods**:
  - Local venue scouting
  - Social media monitoring (TikTok, YouTube, Spotify)
  - Demo submissions
  - Talent competitions
  - Poaching from competitors

#### Artist Attributes
- **Musical Skills**: Vocal ability, instrumental skills, songwriting
- **Performance**: Stage presence, charisma, audience connection
- **Business**: Professionalism, reliability, marketing appeal
- **Personal**: Ego, loyalty, work ethic, substance issues
- **Genre Specialties**: Pop, Rock, Hip-Hop, R&B, Country, Electronic, etc.

#### Development Activities
- **Studio Sessions**: Recording, production, mixing, mastering
- **Performance Training**: Voice coaches, stage presence, choreography
- **Media Training**: Interviews, social media, public speaking
- **Collaboration**: Featuring on other tracks, guest appearances

### 3. Production & Studio Management

#### Equipment Progression
- **Mixing Boards**: From basic 8-track to professional 64-channel consoles
- **Studio Quality**: Home studio → Professional studio → Multiple facilities
- **Equipment Maintenance**: Regular upkeep, upgrades, insurance

#### Production Team
- **Producers**: Different styles, reputations, costs
- **Engineers**: Sound, mixing, mastering specialists
- **Session Musicians**: Hired talent for specific projects
- **Songwriters**: In-house or contracted writers

### 4. Tour Management System

#### Venue Progression Tiers

##### UK Venues
1. **Grassroots (100-500 capacity)**
   - Local pubs, small clubs
   - The Windmill (Brixton), Night & Day Café (Manchester)
   
2. **Small Venues (500-1,500)**
   - The Scala (London), The Leadmill (Sheffield)
   - Thekla (Bristol), King Tut's (Glasgow)

3. **Mid-Tier (1,500-5,000)**
   - Roundhouse (London), Albert Hall (Manchester)
   - O2 Academy venues nationwide

4. **Large Venues (5,000-15,000)**
   - Eventim Apollo (London), P&J Live (Aberdeen)
   - M&S Bank Arena (Liverpool)

5. **Arenas (15,000-25,000)**
   - O2 Arena (London), First Direct Arena (Leeds)
   - Motorpoint Arena (Nottingham)

6. **Stadiums (25,000+)**
   - Wembley Stadium, Old Trafford, Principality Stadium

##### US Venues
1. **Grassroots (100-500)**
   - CBGB-style clubs, local bars
   - The Troubadour (LA), Mercury Lounge (NYC)

2. **Small Venues (500-2,000)**
   - 9:30 Club (DC), The Fillmore (SF)
   - House of Blues venues

3. **Mid-Tier (2,000-8,000)**
   - Beacon Theatre (NYC), Greek Theatre (LA)
   - Red Rocks Amphitheatre (Colorado)

4. **Large Venues (8,000-20,000)**
   - Radio City Music Hall, The Forum (LA)
   - United Center (Chicago)

5. **Arenas (20,000-25,000)**
   - Madison Square Garden, Staples Center
   - TD Garden (Boston)

6. **Stadiums (25,000+)**
   - MetLife Stadium, Rose Bowl, Soldier Field

#### Tour Economics
- **Revenue Streams**: Ticket sales, merchandise, VIP packages, sponsorships
- **Costs**: Venue rental, equipment, crew, transport, accommodation
- **Tour Support**: Label investment in breaking even or profitable tours
- **Routing Optimization**: Efficient travel, local demand analysis

### 5. Chart & Success Metrics

#### Chart Systems
- **Billboard Hot 100** (US Singles)
- **Billboard 200** (US Albums)
- **UK Official Charts** (Singles & Albums)
- **Streaming Charts**: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube
- **Genre-Specific Charts**: Country, R&B, Rock, etc.

#### Success Factors
- **Radio Play**: Different station tiers, payola concerns
- **Streaming Performance**: Platform-specific algorithms
- **Social Media Buzz**: TikTok trends, Twitter mentions
- **Critical Reception**: Review scores, industry recognition

#### Major Awards (2025 Calendar)
- **January**: Critics' Choice Awards, Sundance Film Festival
- **February**: Grammy Awards, BRIT Awards
- **March**: SXSW Festival, iHeartRadio Music Awards
- **April**: Coachella, Country Music Awards

### 7. Financial Management

#### Revenue Streams
- **Record Sales**: Physical, digital, streaming royalties
- **Publishing**: Songwriting, sync licensing, covers
- **Touring**: Concert tickets, merchandise, VIP experiences
- **Partnerships**: Endorsements, brand collaborations
- **Catalog Sales**: Rights auctions, back catalog licensing

#### Expenses
- **Production Costs**: Studio time, equipment, personnel
- **Marketing**: Advertising, promotion, PR campaigns
- **Operations**: Staff salaries, office rent, legal fees
- **Artist Advances**: Signing bonuses, living expenses
- **Tour Support**: Venue deposits, equipment rental

#### Contract Types
- **Traditional Deals**: Label owns masters, high royalty split
- **360 Deals**: Label shares in all revenue streams
- **Distribution Deals**: Artist retains ownership, label distributes
- **Joint Ventures**: Shared ownership and investment

### 8. A&R Department

#### Scouting Network
- **Regional Scouts**: Different territories and specialties
- **Genre Specialists**: Experts in specific musical styles
- **Data Analysts**: Streaming trends, social media metrics
- **Relationship Managers**: Industry connections, referrals

#### Signing Decisions
- **Market Analysis**: Current trends, competition assessment
- **Financial Projections**: Investment vs. return potential
- **Roster Balance**: Genre diversity, release scheduling
- **Development Timeline**: Short-term vs. long-term projects

### 9. Marketing & Promotion

#### Campaign Types
- **Album Cycles**: Pre-release, launch, sustaining momentum
- **Single Pushes**: Radio promotion, playlist placement
- **Tour Marketing**: Venue partnerships, local media
- **Brand Partnerships**: Product placements, endorsements

#### Media Channels
- **Traditional**: Radio, TV, print magazines
- **Digital**: Streaming platforms, social media, influencers
- **Live**: Concerts, festivals, showcase events
- **Alternative**: Podcast appearances, documentary features

### 10. Competitive Elements

#### Industry Rivals
- **Major Labels**: Universal, Sony, Warner
- **Independent Labels**: Competing for the same artists
- **Direct Competition**: Other player-controlled labels
- **Industry Disruption**: New platforms, changing consumer habits

#### Market Dynamics
- **Genre Trends**: Rising and falling musical styles
- **Technology Impact**: New platforms, distribution methods
- **Economic Factors**: Recession effects, consumer spending
- **Cultural Shifts**: Social movements, generational changes

## Success Metrics & End Game

### Victory Conditions
- **Financial**: Reach $1 billion label valuation
- **Cultural**: Dominate multiple chart categories
- **Legacy**: Build sustainable multi-generational success
- **Innovation**: Pioneer new industry practices

### Long-term Gameplay
- **Catalog Management**: Maintaining relevance of back catalog
- **Next Generation**: Discovering and developing new talent
- **Industry Evolution**: Adapting to technological changes
- **Global Expansion**: International market penetration

This roadmap provides the foundation for a comprehensive music industry simulation that captures the complexity and excitement of running a record label from startup to global empire.
